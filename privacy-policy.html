Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This page explains what information DREAMABOVE ("we", "us") collects through this website, how we use it, and who we share it with. This is a plain-English summary and is not a substitute for legal advice.

What we collect

When you submit our site survey / quotation form, we collect the information you provide, which may include:

  • Full name
  • Phone / WhatsApp number
  • Email address
  • City / area
  • Room type, approximate ceiling area, finish preference and lighting details you enter in the estimator
  • Your preferred contact method

We also use website analytics tools, which may automatically collect general usage data such as pages visited and approximate device/browser information.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule a free site survey
  • To provide a project quotation
  • To contact you via your preferred method (WhatsApp, Viber, Messenger, phone or email)

We do not sell your personal information.

Analytics and advertising measurement

This website uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use the site and to measure the performance of our advertising. This may involve cookies and similar technologies. Where we track a successful form submission as a conversion event, we do not send your name, email, phone number, or other personally identifiable information as part of that event.

Form processing

Inquiry form submissions on this site are technically processed through a third-party form service (Formspree). Your submitted details are delivered to our team through that service in order to respond to your inquiry.

Data sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except the service providers described above (form processing and analytics), which are necessary to operate this website and respond to your inquiry.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you submitted by contacting us using the details below.
